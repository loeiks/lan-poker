# No Limit, Texas Holdem Poker

This document explains the rules of "No Limit Texas Holdem Poker" that is most common type of poker also played with friends as well, this document explains the rules just as in casinos.

## General Game Rules

There must be at laest 2 players in the game, for maximum fun its better to be at least 4-5 players or more. Each player has positions that rotates after each game and positions are very important in poker because you are forced to act early sometimes with less information.

> Positions of players are changing after each game, going towards clock-wise from the dealer's position, so rotates through left of dealer.

There is a player called "Button" or "Dealer" which is shown with a disk that says "DEALER" on top of it. And game starts from the left (clock-wise) of that position which are called as **Small Blind** (SB) and **Big Blind** (BB).

BTN, SB, BB.

Next up we will have positions with names of Early, Mid and Late which doesn't really matter in terms of meaning but matters because as we go close to late the chance of winning increases and chance of getting a better hand is also increases.

Each game starts with a forced betting, players in SB and BB positions are forced to bet. SB means 1/2 of minimum bet and BB means the minimum bet. That minimum is depends on table it could be $1, $5 or even more.

After SB and BB positions bets in, the game actually starts.

### Pre Flop

This round called as pre-flop, before that 3 card is revealed there is a betting happening starting from the player next to BB, because SB and BB positions already placed their bets.

This player (left to BB) called also under the gun (UTG), and he/she has to either match at least the minimum bet (same as BB's blind bet), raise it, or fold. In the current and upcoming betting moments players have three options:

Call: match the minimum bet.
Raise: raise the minimum bet.
Fold: throw cards away and fold.

After every player on this pre-flop betting phase bets the equal amounts 3 cards are revealed to everyone and next round starts.

### Flop

Now there are 3 cards shared among players so each player has 5 cards in total and new betting round begins. But this time first player to act has option to **check** this moves action to next player without any bets placed.

Check: just move action to next player without any bets placed.

If all players checks there won't be any new bets and this round will end like that, but if someone bets just like previous rounds everyone has to match the same amount of bet within the round or just fold.

After this round another new 4th card place face up onto table, and new rounds begin.

### The Turn

Just like the previous round no change, bets placed or all checks etc. After this round last card which is the 5th card placed onto table face up.

### The River

Now everyone has 7 cards in total, 5 shared on table and 2 hole cards for each player. And final bets are placed just like previous rounds and when everyone matches the same amount of bet within the round, all players reveal their cards and winner is decided based on strongest hand.

## FAQ

Now let's answer some general questions;

Q: What happens if I don't have the enough amount to call but I want to bet?
A: This means you have to all-in with everything you have and play the game until it completes.

Q: Can I just increase however I want?
A: No you can increase using the stepper which is the minimum bet, for example if minimum is $10, you can't increase to $15, you have to increase to $20, or $30.

Q: How does delaer position determined?
A: It's random up to you, however you want. Just rotate it clock-wise after each game.

Q: What is the minimum bet?
A: Depends on table, default and suggested is $5 or $10 for private games.

Q: When I fold, do I show my cards?
A: No that's againts the rules, you keep them hidden on the table.

Q: When delaing cards to players how do we handle it?
A: You start left of dealer when dealing and deal one card at a time, clock-wise just like playing order.

Q: When placing cards onto table are there any rules?
A: Yes before any card revealed, dealer burns one card face down. (You don't burn 3 card for flop, still 1 before that 3 cards).
